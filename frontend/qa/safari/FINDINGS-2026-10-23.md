relay. Run folder: `qa/safari/results/2026-10-23-final/` (git-ignored;

## Results by topic

**Hard shadow edge below the cards — found on phones, in the card sliders.**
At phone width the exhibition cards sit in a horizontal slider
(`ul.archive-slide-track`, `overflow: auto`). Its scroll box ends about 10 px
above the end of the cards' drop-shadow, so the shadow below every slider card
stops in a straight line (start page and exhibitions page; measured 9.5 px on
iOS 26.5, 11.2 px on iOS 27 and Chromium). It shows in iOS Safari 27 and 26.5
and in Chromium alike: not a Safari bug, but on an iPhone it is where the hard
line appears. Room for the shadow inside the track (bottom padding with a
matching negative margin) would remove it. The gallery cards on /venues/ lose
about 1 px at the sides to `main { overflow: hidden }` — invisible.

**Card shadows on hover (desktop) — no hard edges.** Real Mac Safari with a
real mouse: the exhibition, venue and gallery cards keep a soft shadow at rest,
at 80 ms and 260 ms into the lift, when settled and after the pointer leaves.
The shadow-edge measure below the cards stays at 1–2 (of 255), the same as
Chromium. iOS 27 and 26.5 draw the forced hover state the same way. No parent
`overflow` or `clip-path` cuts a visible shadow on desktop.

**One real Safari difference: Safari rounds shadow sizes to whole pixels.**
Real Safari (Mac 26.6, iOS 26.5 and 27) reports `drop-shadow(… 0 1px 1px)`
for the icon tiles where the CSS says `0 1px 1.5px`, and `0 8px 9px` for the
cards where it says `0.55rem 0.6rem` (8.8 px, 9.6 px). On the big cards this
is invisible. On the small icon tiles (`--drop-shadow-tile`) the resting
shadow is tighter and darker than in Chrome: edge step ~8 in Safari against
~5 in Chromium, visible as a crisper line under the tile. Whole-pixel values
(for example `0 1px 2px`) would look the same in both.

**Gradients with text — no Safari difference.** Sticky header over scrolled
content, exhibition hero, featured-exhibition overlay, venue preview shade,
selected ledger row: the same in real Safari, the iOS Simulators and Chromium.
The hero wheel buttons (gradient + `clip-path` + `drop-shadow` + blend mode)
have the same shapes; Safari draws their soft shadows a little lighter.

**Fonts — load and draw everywhere; two faked styles.** Cormorant Garamond
300 / 400 / 500 load and really draw in every runner. But the site also asks
for styles that are not loaded, so every browser fakes them:

- **italic** — the quote on the start page, the artist letter tiles, image
  captions ("Abb. I …") on venue and person pages, the quote on venue pages.
- **600 bold** — `font-semibold` in `ArchiveEditorialSection.vue` (About page).

Safari fakes bold differently: the faked-bold name "Adi Schmölzer" on the
About page runs about 6 % wider in Safari than in Chrome (111.5 against
105 px on the Mac, 99.1 against 93.4 px on the phone), so line breaks can
differ. Loading the real faces (`@fontsource/cormorant-garamond/400-italic.css`,
`600.css`) or using 500 instead of 600 would make all browsers match.

**Phone width: an endless ResizeObserver loop on /venues/ — every browser.**
At iPhone size the venues page throws "ResizeObserver loop completed with
undelivered notifications" 60+ times per visit, in iOS Safari 27 and 26.5,
WebKit and Chromium alike. Likely cause (not yet proven):
`VenueMaskedImage.vue` writes its measured size into the SVG `viewBox`; when
the SVG's height follows that viewBox, every update changes the size again.

**360° tour modal — opens and closes in every Safari.** First canvas after
0.5–0.6 s, no error card, closed cleanly. On iPhone (and with a script click)
there is no fullscreen; the modal covers the page by CSS, as designed. The
tour's own travel and large-image behaviour is the tour viewer's review.

**Static check, Safari 18 / 26 / 27.** No CSS or JS that breaks Safari 18.
Only cosmetic gaps: `scrollbar-width` (Safari 18.2+), `scrollbar-color`,
`accent-color` and dotted underlines (Safari 26.2+). Details: `audit-css.md`,
`audit-js.md` in the run folder.

## Kit lessons

- Mac Safari passes the driver's mouse moves to the page only while its
  window has focus. The driver now brings Safari to the front before each
  move; without it, no hover happens and the pictures only show the rest state.
- In the Simulator, the status bar's battery icon is green, like a
  calibration mark; the green mark is searched below the magenta one.

## Suggested next steps (owner's decision)

1. Give the phone card sliders room for the shadow (`archive-slide-track`).
2. Tile shadow in whole pixels (`--drop-shadow-tile`, maybe the card shadows too).
3. Load Cormorant italic and 600, or drop `font-semibold` to 500.
4. Fix the ResizeObserver loop in `VenueMaskedImage.vue` on /venues/ at phone width.
5. Real devices: `pnpm qa:safari:serve --lan`, then open the printed address
   on an iPhone and an iPad with a trackpad (real hover on iOS).
6. Safari 18 and Mac Safari 27: a cloud browser service (BrowserStack,
   LambdaTest), or the iOS 18 Simulator from developer.apple.com, or update this
   Mac's Safari to 27 for the next run.
