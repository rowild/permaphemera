## PermaPhemera is online

Hello,

thank you for subscribing to the PermaPhemera newsletter. This first issue shows you what
the archive already holds.

[permaphemera.at](https://permaphemera.at/en/) is an archive of exhibitions in Austrian
galleries. Each exhibition is captured as a 360° tour, with the consent of the gallery or venue
and of the artists, and stays online after it is taken down: the whole room, the works as they
were hung, with the sight lines between the works.

## The archive in numbers

- **26 exhibitions**, **18 of them as 360° tours** that open in the browser
- **72 artists**, each with a page and their exhibitions in the archive
- **15 galleries and exhibition venues** in **2 federal states**
- **9 organisations**: associations, initiatives and institutions that run galleries or
  organise exhibitions

Every venue with its address and exhibitions: [permaphemera.at/en/venues/](https://permaphemera.at/en/venues/)

## What the website offers

- **Exhibitions** by current, upcoming and past, searchable by title, artist, gallery, town
  and date: [/en/exhibitions/](https://permaphemera.at/en/exhibitions/)
- **360° tours** on the page of each exhibition, on a computer and on a phone
- **Artists** from A to Z: [/en/artists/](https://permaphemera.at/en/artists/)
- **News**: openings, last days of exhibitions and news about the website:
  [/en/news/](https://permaphemera.at/en/news/)
- Everything in German and English

## For galleries

Would you like to suggest an exhibition for a 360° tour, add to an entry or correct it? You are
welcome to write via [permaphemera.at/en/contact/](https://permaphemera.at/en/contact/).
