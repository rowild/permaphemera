# kultur.im.puls: complete the organisation (2026-11-11)

Owner's request: research the Verein behind the Galerie im Stiegenhaus and put it on
PermaPhemera with logo, images, names and roles, address and websites. The organisation
existed already (published, `kultur-im-puls`, since 2026-10-14) without logo, photo, address
and members, so it is completed, not created. Owner's choice: all 12 people of the page
"Vorstand und Team" become members.

Folder: `plans/2026-11-11__kultur-im-puls/` (worklog, manifest, SEO file, research files).

## Decisions

- Address: the gallery, `Impuls Center, Thomas Morgenstern Platz 1, 9871 Seeboden am
  Millstätter See, Austria`. The postal address in the imprint is the chair's home address
  (c/o Caro Meinl, Spittal); a private home address is not published.
- Logo: `Logo-Kultur-impuls.png` (525 × 146, the 2x file of the site header) in `logo`.
- Photo: the group photo from the article, cropped out of the scan (1180 × 845): the new board
  with the departing members who received honorary membership. Credit as printed:
  "Foto: kultur.im.puls Seeboden". Only Carolin Meinl is named in the caption (front).
- Functions in German as the sources write them: the article's forms where it has one
  (Obfrau, Stellvertreter, Schriftführerinnen, Kassier, Ausstellungsorganisatorin), else the
  website's words; "Kassier-Stellvertretung" for "Kassier Stv." (the site gives no form).
- Role `artist` only for the two people with a source that calls them artists (Melanie Joch,
  Gerhart Weihs). Wolfgang Daborer exists already (artist, published).
- New persons are drafts; memberships have no status and appear when a person is published.
- New links on the organisation: artothek-impuls.at (the association's Artothek platform, named
  in the article) and the YouTube channel linked from kultur-impuls.com.
